Questions? We've Got Answers

Does it really clean inside the ear canal?

That is the whole point of the curved applicator. It reaches past the flap and into the canal, where the wax actually sits. You massage the base for about thirty seconds, your pet shakes the loosened build-up back out, and you wipe it away instead of pushing it further down.

How is this different from a gut supplement?

They do completely different jobs. A supplement works from the inside and needs months before you can judge it. AMBLORA works in the ear canal itself, where the problem sits. It rinses out the wax, the gunk and the smell that cotton balls and wipes leave behind. You see the difference the same evening, not next season.

Can I use it on my cat?

Yes, the same bottle is made for both. A cat simply needs fewer drops than a large dog. If you have a dog and a cat at home, one bottle covers the whole house.

Is it safe if he licks his paw after?

Yes. There is no alcohol in the formula, which is why it does not sting going in and why it is safe if your pet licks it afterwards. Leaving alcohol out was a deliberate choice, not a shortcut.

How often should I use it?

Twice a week is enough for most pets, and it takes under a minute. Floppy-eared breeds and dogs that swim tend to need it more regularly, since a warm closed canal is exactly where wax and yeast like to sit.

How fast will I see a difference?

You see the first rinse work right away, because the loosened build-up comes out when your pet shakes his head and you wipe it off. The head shaking and the smell usually settle over the first few days of regular use.
