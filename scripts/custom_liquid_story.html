PLACEMENT 01 / THE HOLIDAY ENTRANCE

A Warm Welcome at the Front Door.

Create an inviting holiday entrance by placing the gnome beside a Christmas wreath, evergreen garland or decorated porch chair.

The long hat and star-shaped lantern draw attention, while the forest greenery complements traditional outdoor Christmas styling.

Use a sheltered, dry location unless the manufacturer's instructions confirm weather resistance.

Beside the Christmas Tree.

Position the gnome beside the tree, near wrapped gifts or next to a festive basket filled with greenery and decorative pinecones.

His warm star lantern complements the surrounding tree lights, creating a coordinated holiday atmosphere without needing excessive decoration.

He can also sit near a seasonal display cabinet or a low, stable decorative console.

MORE WAYS TO DECORATE

Make Space for a Little Christmas Wonder.

You don't need an elaborate Christmas arrangement to enjoy this character. A thoughtfully chosen spot and a few complementary decorations can create a beautiful scene.

In the Entryway

Set him on a stable console or low decorative surface beside a holiday sign, greenery or a basket of Christmas ornaments. His glowing star can welcome guests as they enter.

Beside the Fireplace

Display him near decorative stockings, garlands and wrapped presents for a cozy cottage look. Keep him well away from open flames, heaters and hot surfaces.

Near the Staircase

A stable landing or hallway display can make a charming spot, especially with nearby Christmas garlands. Keep stairs, walkways and handrails completely unobstructed.

In a Christmas Display Corner

Pair him with miniature trees, rustic lanterns, wrapped gift boxes and a few simple ornaments for a woodland-themed arrangement in a living room or family space.

On a Sheltered Porch

Create a welcoming scene next to evergreen planters, a wreath or Christmas ribbon. Protect the decoration from moisture unless outdoor suitability is confirmed.

As a Thoughtful Christmas Gift

A festive decorative gift for someone who loves gnomes, traditional Christmas homes, woodland decorations and warm holiday lighting.

Use on a secure, stable surface. Keep the lantern and any electrical components away from moisture and heat, and follow the manufacturer's power and placement instructions.
