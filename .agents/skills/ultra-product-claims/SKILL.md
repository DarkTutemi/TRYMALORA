# ULTRA OVERNIGHT: Evidence-Gated Product Claims

## Version lock
V4 FULL-SPINE ULTRA OVERNIGHT is the flagship. V1/V2/V3 are historic research. Seven design pillars are Ultra-Lock Core, Full-Spine Shield, SideSeal Guard, DryTouch, Odor Control, CloudFlex, WashGuard. Factory concept agreed **per owner** does not equal sample passing tests.

## Claim ledger
For every statement record: `claim_id`, text, feature, intended placement, evidence type, source artifact link, test protocol, sample size, measurement units, date, competitor comparability, review owner and status.
Statuses:
- `DESIGN_TARGET`: engineering objective only; must be labelled as target (not asserted as performance).
- `FACTORY_ASSERTED`: supplier verbal/documented capability, not independent validation.
- `SAMPLE_MEASURED`: observed sample measurement with procedure and condition.
- `LAB_VALIDATED`: lab report with method, sample IDs and results; verify authenticity.
- `MARKETING_APPROVED`: wording supported by evidence, scope and legal review where appropriate.
- `BLOCKED`: insufficient/contradictory evidence; never publish as fact.

## Specific proposed claims
- 150–160 mL: **DESIGN_TARGET** only. Do not say “holds 160 mL” or “beats Revol Cares” without tested capacity under comparable methods.
- 360°: **design intent** for coverage; never imply physically leakproof from all directions unless proven with repeatable side/back tests.
- 50–100 washes: target for functional durability, not a blanket lifespan warranty.
- DryTouch, SideSeal, Odor Control, rewet/retention and CloudFlex: test and describe only measured or plainly structural features.
- `period proof`, “no leaks ever”, “100% protection”, medical/antimicrobial endorsements require independent strong substantiation; avoid false absolutes.

## Suggested evidence checks
1. Capacity to leakage / failure threshold in mL, realistic menstrual fluid analogue and flow rate; define if unpressurized saturation or wear-relevant.
2. Rewet mass under defined pressure and dwell time.
3. Side-sleeping/back-gap leakage on fit forms with several sizes and positions; photograph seam placement.
4. Absorbent core thickness when dry and after wetting; comfort & heat profile.
5. Performance after 0, 10, 25, 50, 100 wash cycles, with wash/dry instructions held constant.
6. Odor: blinded panel or validated chemical/odor measure; avoid guessing antibacterial effect.
7. Competitor comparison requires the same protocol, comparable size and independent samples. Rival marketed capacity alone is not comparable laboratory data.

## Copy gate
Write benefits strongly when verifiable ("designed for...", "engineered with..."). Use placeholders for missing test figures, never synthetic lab results, fake social proof, fabricated reviews or unsupported refunds guarantees.
