# ULTRA OVERNIGHT: Brand Voice and Research

Use existing ECC `brand-voice`, `brand-discovery`, `market-research` as background research only; do not activate unrelated ECC runtime/hooks automatically.

## Voice principles
- US English, confident, empathetic, calm, modern, human. Invite rather than shame. Respect menstruation as normal.
- Value: more restful-feeling nights, comfort, less worry; do not promise uninterrupted sleep, zero leaks or physiological outcomes without test evidence.
- Founder/merchant name, contact details, return address and legal identity must be confirmed; don't invent a founder story or imply the payment descriptor changes just because the site rebrands.
- Product identity: **ULTRA OVERNIGHT** with V4/Full-Spine as internal engineering nomenclature when useful; avoid auto-translating to old TRYMALORA identity.

## Editorial checklist
1. Hero: concise problem, benefit, legitimate specific differentiator, one action.
2. About Us: why product was developed, design principles and factory process; do not fabricate certifications or manufacture location.
3. Product: explain 5-layer structure and seven pillars at an evidence-appropriate level; label targets as targets before validation.
4. FAQ: absorption, backup use, sizing, washing, fabric care, shipping, refunds/unused hygiene returns and tracking; policies must reflect actual store terms.
5. Metadata: title, description, OpenGraph, alt text, logo, favicon, structured data; confirm no Trymalora remnants or other product labels in flagship routes.
6. Navigation: Home, Shop ULTRA OVERNIGHT, About, Track Order, FAQ/Contact; no legacy Shop All.
7. Maintain canonical URL and brand/legal distinctions; native Shopify checkout and product receipts may still show merchant details.

## Copy deliverable format
Primary option, alternative options, claim ledger links, evidence notes, and testable CTA hypothesis. Never assert the product's real-world benefits beyond substantiation.
