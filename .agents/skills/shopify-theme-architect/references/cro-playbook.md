# E-Commerce Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Playbook

Practical conversion principles adapted for Shopify storefronts and product detail pages.

## 1. Visual Merchandising & Hierarchy
1. **Above-the-Fold Value Proposition:** Communicate the primary outcome, core visual proof, price, and primary Add-to-Cart path without forcing excessive scrolling.
2. **Image Hierarchy:** Lead with a crisp hero product shot showing scale and usage context, followed by detail close-ups, lifestyle benefits, and packaging/dimensions. Allow click-to-zoom on small upsell thumbnails so shoppers can inspect craftsmanship clearly.
3. **Whitespace = Focus:** Give CTAs and pricing blocks clean breathing room; overcrowded badges reduce trust and comprehension.
4. **F-Pattern Scanning:** Align ratings, headline, benefits, pricing, bundle selector, and primary CTA down one clear visual axis.

## 2. Pricing, Bundles & Average Order Value (AOV)
5. **Price Anchoring & Bundle Math:** Show exact per-unit economics (`$59.99 / ea`) alongside verified compare-at prices so multi-pack savings are immediately intuitive.
6. **Pre-Selected Recommended Tier:** Highlight the most popular value bundle cleanly (`Most Popular`) while letting single-unit buyers choose Buy 1 frictionlessly.
7. **Contextual Companion Upsells:** Place relevant add-ons right above the Add-to-Cart CTA with a single checkbox + click-to-zoom preview + instant live total recalculation.
8. **Shipping Threshold Feedback:** Show real cart progress toward free shipping thresholds dynamically when selecting bundles or upsells.

## 3. Copy & Trust Engineering
9. **Benefit-First Copy:** Translate technical specs into customer outcomes (`Warm-white LED glow creates a welcoming porch ambiance` rather than raw SKU specs).
10. **Objection Preemption:** Place shipping expectations, return policy (`30-Day Money-Back Guarantee`), and top FAQs close to the purchase decision area.
11. **Truthful Urgency & Proof Only:** Never fabricate scarcity, stock counts, live viewer numbers, fake reviews, or unverified claims. Authenticity drives sustainable conversion and protects store trust.
12. **Reading Clarity:** Keep bullet points scannable (under ~14 words per bullet) and avoid walls of unbroken text.

## 4. Mobile Shopping & Cart Friction Reduction
13. **Mobile Thumb Zone:** Place primary Buy Box CTAs and mobile Sticky ATC cleanly within comfortable thumb reach.
14. **Direct Cart Integration:** Keep `/cart/add.js` payloads synchronized for multi-item bundles + upsells + line-item properties, updating the cart drawer cleanly without broken redirects or dead clicks.
15. **Speed & Core Web Vitals:** Lazy-load below-the-fold review/lifestyle images while keeping hero media eager with explicit `width`/`height` attributes to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
