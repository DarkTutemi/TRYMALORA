## 1. Regulatory Scope (United States)
- **Product:** CERULINE AFM/DFM Disabler (OBD-II Plug-and-Play Active Fuel Management / Dynamic Fuel Management Disabler).
- **Applicable Regulations:**
  1. **EPA Clean Air Act (CAA) — Section 203(a)(3):** Prohibits tampering with or defeating emissions control systems on motor vehicles. Disabling cylinder deactivation (AFM/DFM) alters fuel/emissions calibration behavior.
  2. **CARB (California Air Resources Board):** Aftermarket OBD-II devices sold in California that affect emissions-related systems typically require a valid **CARB Executive Order (EO)** number.
